The Arthur

Three ways to underwrite the same motel. The buttons change the case. The comparison at the top stays put, because that is the decision.

The 45-key case does not clear a normal development test. A studs-out rebuild at the marketing plan’s 68% occupancy and $375 rate costs about $27.9 million and earns about $1.3 million of stabilized profit. That is a 4.7% yield, and the permanent loan comes up about $5.5 million short. The case a lender can take is 96 keys.

Yield on cost

Stabilized profit divided by total project cost. The exit cap used to value the hotel is 7.5%. The development hurdle used here is 9%.

Purchase price is $9.8 million in every case. A positive refinance is cash returned at the start of year 3. A negative number is extra equity the deal still needs. The return treats that shortfall as a cash call.
